# Contribute

SciVisAI is a community resource, shaped and sustained by the people who use it. Community contributions help the site reflect the needs, experience, and perspectives of the scientific visualization community.

<div class="notice" style="border-left: 4px solid #369E88; background: #f2f8f6; padding: 1rem 1.25rem; margin: 1.5rem 0;">

<strong>Interested in contributing to the SciVisAI site?</strong>

Have an idea for a topic, want to add to a page, or hoping to request an edit? Reach out to <a href="mailto:s.saharan@utoronto.ca">s.saharan@utoronto.ca</a> with all contributions and requests.

</div>

Ideas might include technical experiments and case studies, tool reviews, workflow documentation, teaching materials, edits to existing content, or reports of inaccuracies.
